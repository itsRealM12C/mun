MUN extractor

A client-side miner for Windows .mun files (Multi-User Resource modules, Windows 10 RS5+). Walks the PE resource tree, decodes icons, bitmaps, PNGs, JPEGs, cursors, version info, manifest, and the MUI split-file descriptor (0xFECDFECD). Reconstructs .ico and .cur files from RT_GROUP_ICON / RT_GROUP_CURSOR + their leaves. Nothing is uploaded — it all runs in your browser.
